Step 1 · About 10 minutes

🧭 Check the Website Address

Look at the end of a website address. It gives you a clue.

  • 🏛 .gov = Made by the government. Usually true.
  • 🎓 .edu = Made by a school. Usually true.
  • 🤝 .org = Made by a group. Usually good. Check who pays for it.
  • 🏢 .com = Made by a business. Could be good or bad. Check first.

Step 2 · About 10 minutes

🏛 Find News Near You

Want to know what is happening near you? Try these places first:

  • 🏢 Your city website — real news and events.
  • 📚 Your public library — free classes and events.
  • 📻 Your local newspaper or radio station.
  • ☎️ Call 211 — a free phone line for help near you.

Step 3 · About 10 minutes

🩺 Health Information

When you read health news, look for good signs and warning signs.

  • ✅ Good sign: words like "doctors," "hospital," "study," or "CDC."
  • ⚠️ Warning sign: words like "miracle," "secret," or "cures everything."

Step 4 · About 10 minutes

📰 Check Before You Trust

Before you believe a news story, try this:

  • 1️⃣ Open a new tab.
  • 2️⃣ Search the name of the website.
  • 3️⃣ See what other people say about it.
